Making room for play, wherever you celebrate.

A few thoughtful questions to ask before choosing activities for your celebration.

Sample content for design approval. This story has not been published by Brinks.

Start with the space

Before choosing the games, take a look at where the celebration will happen. Is there room for movement? Is there a quieter area? What happens if outdoor plans need to move indoors?

Think about the group

Share the approximate number and ages of the children with your entertainment team. A mixed-age group may need a different rhythm from a small group of friends of the same age.

Leave a little breathing room

Food, cake, arrivals and goodbyes all need their own space in the day. An entertainment plan works best when it fits the celebration rather than competing with it.
