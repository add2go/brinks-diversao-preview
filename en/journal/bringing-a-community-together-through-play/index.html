Big celebrations start with little connections.

A welcoming approach to planning family moments at a community event.

Sample content for design approval. This story has not been published by Brinks.

Begin with a warm welcome

At a community celebration, children may be meeting for the first time. A friendly introduction and an easy first activity can make joining in feel more comfortable.

Offer different ways to take part

Some children are ready to move. Others prefer to watch before joining. Discuss a plan that leaves room for different levels of confidence and participation.

Keep the purpose in view

The aim is to create a shared experience for families. Tell your entertainment team what kind of event you are planning and what you would like people to remember.
