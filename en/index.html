A few things you might be wondering

Little questions.
Helpful answers.

Where do you offer entertainment?+

Brinks is based in Greater Vancouver, British Columbia. Share your event location when you request a quote so the team can confirm availability and travel details.

Can I combine games and face painting?+

Tell us which experiences you are interested in. The team can discuss a combination and confirm the scope in your personalised quote.

What do you need to prepare a quote?+

Your event date, city, approximate number and ages of children, and preferred activities are a helpful starting point. Duration, staffing and requirements will be confirmed together.

Do you speak English and Portuguese?+

You can explore this website in English or Portuguese. Let us know your preferred language when discussing your event.

How do I book?+

Start with a quote request. Availability, package details and booking terms are confirmed by the team before a booking is made.
